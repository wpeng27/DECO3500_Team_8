**Participant 1**

1. *How did the activity affect how comfortable you felt talking to unfamiliar participants? Can you describe a particular moment?*

It made talking easier because we had something to do together. When the hidden-cards rule came up, we had to help each other choose cards, which gave us a reason to talk and laugh without needing a formal introduction.

2. *How did the questions and changing UNO rules affect your conversations? Did they help conversations develop or interrupt them?*

The questions helped us move beyond talking about the game. The changing rules kept things interesting, although sometimes we stopped a conversation to work out what the new rule meant. Changing rules during a short break could make that smoother.

3. *What was it like using your phone to join, answer or skip questions, and receive match alerts? How did it affect your attention to the other players and shared screen?*

Answering on my phone was convenient because I didn’t have to reach across the shared screen. A QR code on the main screen would make joining easier than opening a shared link manually. The match alerts were useful, but I occasionally checked my phone while someone was speaking.

4. *What did you understand the changing colours and final match results to mean? How, if at all, did they influence who you wanted to talk to or work with?*

I understood that our colours became closer when our answers matched. That was quite cool and gave me a reason to ask what we had in common. I treated the final results as a starting point for talking, rather than a definite recommendation about who to work with.

5. *How comfortable did you feel choosing what to share or whether to participate? What would you change to make the experience better?*

Being able to skip questions made me feel comfortable. I liked joining as a guest too. I would add the QR code and make it clearer at the start that skipping does not count against you.

**Participant 3**

1. *How did the activity affect how comfortable you felt talking to unfamiliar participants? Can you describe a particular moment?*

It felt more natural than being told to introduce ourselves. Negotiating a card swap gave me an easy way to speak to another player, and the conversation became more relaxed after that.

5. *How comfortable did you feel choosing what to share or whether to participate? What would you change to make the experience better?*

The guest and skip options helped me feel in control. Some multiple-choice answers didn’t quite fit me, so I would like an “Other” option or a chance to explain aloud. I would also add a simple colour legend so we could understand the feedback without interrupting the game.
