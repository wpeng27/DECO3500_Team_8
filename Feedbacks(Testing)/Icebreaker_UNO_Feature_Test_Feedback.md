# Icebreaker UNO Feature Test Feedback

## Participant 1

### 1. Did the activity make talking to unfamiliar students easier or harder?

Easier. Playing gave us something to talk about without having to introduce ourselves formally.

### 2. How did the optional questions affect conversation? Were you comfortable not answering?

They helped us find things in common. Knowing I could skip made it comfortable.

### 3. What did you think the wristband colours meant? How did they affect who you talked to?

I understood them as shared interests. They gave me something to ask another player about.

### 4. What did the digital features add or take away compared with ordinary UNO?

The shared screen made it easier to follow whose turn it was.

### 5. What should change before the final prototype?

Keep the joining process simple. That helped us get started quickly.

## Participant 2

### 1. Did the activity make talking to unfamiliar students easier or harder?

I felt less awkward because everyone was focused on the same activity.

### 2. How did the optional questions affect conversation? Were you comfortable not answering?

One answer led to a conversation about games we both played. I felt comfortable having the option to skip.

### 3. What did you think the wristband colours meant? How did they affect who you talked to?

I wasn’t sure about the difference between colour and shade at first. A small explanation would help.

### 4. What did the digital features add or take away compared with ordinary UNO?

The questions added conversations that probably wouldn’t happen during ordinary UNO.

### 5. What should change before the final prototype?

Add a short explanation of the wristband colours and shades.

## Participant 3

### 1. Did the activity make talking to unfamiliar students easier or harder?

The game helped me relax, and I started talking more after a few turns.

### 2. How did the optional questions affect conversation? Were you comfortable not answering?

The questions were easy to answer, and I liked being able to change them.

### 3. What did you think the wristband colours meant? How did they affect who you talked to?

They made me curious about what interests we shared, but I still talked to everyone.

### 4. What did the digital features add or take away compared with ordinary UNO?

I occasionally looked at my phone and missed someone speaking. A clearer notification could help.

### 5. What should change before the final prototype?

Make question notifications easier to notice so I don’t keep checking my phone.
