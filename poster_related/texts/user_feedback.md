User feedback

Participants described UNO with questions as more relaxed than formal introductions or questions alone. The shared activity made silences less awkward, while optional questions helped students discover common interests. Participants valued being able to skip questions, and colour-based similarity cues provided additional conversation starters.

However, playing cards and checking multiple screens sometimes distracted participants from listening, and having the tutor nearby made some participants feel assessed. Suggested improvements included simplifying the displays, explaining the colour cues more clearly, and allowing short pauses for follow-up conversations.

In response, the final prototype brings all game information onto one shared screen, explains the colour cues on the rules screen, and adds a Pause button that stops the game and the timer so players can keep talking.
