User feedback

Participants described UNO with questions as more relaxed than formal introductions or questions alone. The shared activity made silences less awkward, while optional questions helped students discover common interests. Participants valued being able to skip questions, and colour-based similarity cues provided additional conversation starters.

However, playing cards and checking multiple screens sometimes distracted participants from listening. Suggested improvements included simplifying the displays, explaining the colour cues more clearly, and allowing short pauses for follow-up conversations.
