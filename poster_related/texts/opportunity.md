
## Design Opportunity

When students are asked to self-form assignment groups, the difficulty is not always finding classmates. The more difficult part can be approaching unfamiliar people without knowing whether they already have a group, whether they are open to being approached, or how to start a conversation.

This creates an opportunity to provide a **shared, low-pressure reason** for unfamiliar classmates to interact before deciding whether to form a team.

### The Experience Should Allow Students To:

- Recognise who is currently available to interact;
- Approach others without appearing to interrupt or impose;
- Gradually disclose information rather than revealing private information all at once;
- Notice possible common ground through interaction;
- Leave or continue the interaction without automatically committing to a team.

### Core Design Opportunity

The opportunity is **not to automatically find the “best” teammates**, but to make the first interaction between unfamiliar classmates easier and facilitate further communication.
