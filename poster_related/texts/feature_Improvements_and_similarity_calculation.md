Feature Improvements
- Question themes: Let players choose topics such as hobbies, university life, or teamwork.
- Shared challenges: Introduce short activities, such as finding one interest in common with another player.
- Optional follow-up questions: Allow players to explain their answers and develop longer conversations.
- Post-game conversation suggestions: Use shared answers or complementary roles to suggest topics for further discussion.
- Rule-change previews: Show a countdown and a short explanation before introducing a new rule.

Similarity Calculation
Only questions answered by both players are compared. Skipped or unanswered questions do not affect the score.
- For most questions, identical answers count as a match.
- For the preferred-role question, different answers count as a match because they represent complementary roles.
- With no shared questions, no score is shown.
- With one shared question, both answers are displayed without a percentage.
- With two or more shared questions, the score is calculated as:
similarity = (matching answers + 1) / (shared questions + 2) * 100%

The result is rounded to the nearest whole number. For example, three matches across four shared questions produce a score of 67%. The adjustment prevents a few answers from producing extreme scores.
