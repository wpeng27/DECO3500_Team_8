Problem

Forming groups with unfamiliar classmates can create social pressure and uncertainty. Our interviews revealed concerns about interrupting others, being rejected, and not knowing which groups still have space. Students also had limited information about potential teammates’ communication habits and working styles. As a result, group formation often depended on seating or prior familiarity, leaving some students hesitant to approach others. Some international students also avoided highly capable or fluent classmates because working with them made them feel less confident.
