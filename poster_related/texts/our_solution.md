Our solution

Icebreaker UNO combines a physical card game with a shared-screen web app to give unfamiliar classmates a shared reason to interact. Playing an action card prompts a question about study habits and teamwork preferences, and the app compares answers to highlight similarities. After three questions are answered, the game rules change to encourage further interaction. Students can skip questions or use Guest mode, allowing them to participate at their own comfort level. The session ends after 10 minutes or when one player runs out of cards.
