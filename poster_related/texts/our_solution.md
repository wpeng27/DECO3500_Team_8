Our solution

Icebreaker UNO combines a physical card game with a shared-screen web app and optional phone screens to give unfamiliar classmates a shared reason to interact. Playing an action card prompts a question about study habits and teamwork preferences, and the app compares answers to highlight similarities. Players can also join from their own phones, which show their current colour, let them answer or skip questions, and chime and vibrate when their answer matches another player's. After every three answered questions, the extra game rule changes to encourage further interaction. Students can skip questions or use Guest mode, allowing them to participate at their own comfort level. The session ends after 10 minutes or when one player runs out of cards, and the screen then shows how similar each pair of players' answers were.
