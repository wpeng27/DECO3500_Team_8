Our solution

1.Play and start sharing
Playing an action card triggers a question about study habits or teamwork preferences. These prompts give students a starting point for conversation and help them gradually learn about potential teammates through play.

2.Notice common ground
The app compares answers and uses colour cues to highlight similarities. Optional phones display each player’s current colour and provide sound and vibration alerts when answers match, giving players a reason to talk. Similarity is based only on questions both players answer; skipped or unanswered questions do not affect the score.

3.Make space for conversation
After every three answered questions, an extra game rule changes to encourage different interactions. Players can pause both the game and timer when a conversation develops, allowing time to explain answers and ask follow-up questions.

4.Participate at your own pace
Players can answer or skip questions using the shared screen or their own phones. Guest mode allows participation without submitting interest tags or having answer choices saved, giving students control over what they share.

5.Continue connecting
The session ends after 10 minutes or when one player runs out of cards. A final summary shows similarities between players’ answers as conversation starters. Students can then choose whether to keep talking or form a team.
