Blind Hand - Hold your cards facing outward so others can help you determine which ones to play.
Questions Only - Communication is restricted to questions for a set period (Can one refuse to answer? It is recommended to include a "pass" option, such as shaking one's head to skip).
Gift - Give a card you deem helpful to another player.
Swap - Exchange specific cards or your entire hand with another player when the rules allow.
Team Round - Suspend individual winning goals; the total number of cards held by all players at the table must be reduced below the target level before time runs out.
