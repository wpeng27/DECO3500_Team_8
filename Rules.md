1. **Blind Hand** - Hold your cards facing outward so others can help you determine which ones to play.

2. **Questions Only** - Communication is restricted to questions for a set period. (Can one refuse to answer? It is recommended to include a "pass" option, such as shaking one's head to skip.)

3. **Gift** - Give a card you deem helpful to another player.

4. **Swap** - Exchange specific cards or your entire hand with another player when the rules allow.

5. **Team Round** - Suspend individual winning goals; the total number of cards held by all players
