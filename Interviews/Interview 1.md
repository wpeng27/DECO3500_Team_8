# Observations
- Existing relationships strongly shape group formation.
- Group formation can feel unstructured and unpredictable.
- Students rely on limited visible cues when choosing teammates.
- Previous experience influences how students select group members.
- Motivation and working expectations are important but difficult to know beforehand.
- Students need awareness of whether others have already formed or joined a group.
- Social confidence can influence group choices as much as perceived ability.
- Compatibility involves more than skills, including motivation, expectations, familiarity, and communication comfort.
