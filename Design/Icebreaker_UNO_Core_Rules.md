# Icebreaker UNO — Core Interaction Rules

These are the five core interaction rules we aim to retain in V2. They give players simple opportunities to communicate, help one another and cooperate during physical UNO.

## 1. Blind Hand

Hold your cards facing outward so that other players can see them and help you decide which card to play.

## 2. Questions Only

For a short period, communicate only through questions. Players may pass or use a simple gesture, such as shaking their head, instead of answering.

## 3. Gift

Give one card to another player that you think could help them.

## 4. Swap

Exchange a selected card or your entire hand with another player when the rules allow it.

## 5. Team Round

Temporarily suspend individual winning goals. Players work together towards a shared objective based on the table's combined hand count.

*The exact target and duration of the Team Round are still to be defined.*
